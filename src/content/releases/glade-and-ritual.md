## Help a small bug village save their sacred flower!

_as per the Bandcamp page_
created for the 2026 GMTK Game Jam.
after being asked to assist with sound design part-way into the jam, my work was cut out for me. all music was created in four and half days.

I'm quite proud of what I was able to get done, and really hope you enjoy it!!

game can be found at [netal.itch.io/glade-and-ritual](https://netal.itch.io/glade-and-ritual)

_as per the game page_
All the critters of The Glade lived a peaceful life. In the centre of the village a magic blue flower grew, every night the critters performed a ritual to keep the flower healthy. In return the flower granted them protection from the dangers of the forest floor.

One day, the sacred flower lost a single petal, who's to blame?

...and how many days do they have left?

### Song notes

**Boot Up** was created to help users test their volume. Sadly, we forgot to tell users to adjust their volume.

**Colourless Green** is an introduction to the Glade, and was the first song made for the project.

**The Glade** is the looping music heard through much of the game, helping underscore each character's dialogue.

**Petuliar Circumstances** was not included in the game, but was finished up for this album!

**Mudslinging** describes how the critters that inhabit The Glade have been throwing shade (possibly nightshades?) at eachother throughout the week. The song plays on the final day of the game.

**The Ritual** plays during... well, the ritual. you'll see what i mean.

**Commelina** is the last thing you hear in the game, and is a cute little farewell.
